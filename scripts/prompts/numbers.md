This is a NUMBERS puzzle: the player works out how {{LANGUAGE}} builds number words, NACLO-style (bases other than ten, subtraction, overcounting, multiplication inside words, special forms for some units, number words that change with the counted noun).
- Corpus `lang` lines are number expressions; `en` is the value in digits, e.g. "324". They may count one themed noun ("324 sheep") when the language's number words change with what is counted. Gloss every word, e.g. "three-ten" or "twenty.PL".
- toEnglish: a number expression; accepted answers are the digits, with and without thousands separators where relevant.
- toLang: digits; accepted answers are every correct way to say it.
- Use the system as it is actually used (or was traditionally, if the theme is historical). If modern speakers mix in another system, say so in explain.notes.
- The story lives in the hint, the theme and the counted noun: a market, a tribute list, a shepherd's tally, a calendar.
